Desk Reset 40

Leave with a five-minute movement break built for your desk day.

Product typeOne-off clinic
40 min total Max 8 people ฿590 test price Tue 07:30 candidate

Who buys the job

Desk-based workers near P23—and current clients whose workday is screen-heavy.

The paid difference is not “more stretching.” It is a desk-compatible routine, taught with individual observation, then rehearsed until the customer can use it alone.

Customer owns

A five-minute routine card, two personal movement cues and a clear time-of-day anchor.

It is not

A cure for “office syndrome,” ergonomic assessment, physiotherapy or recurring class commitment.

Reach first

P23 tenants and teams, building channels, then current clients—not a broad paid campaign.

Market signals—not demand proof

  • 37.9% seven-day musculoskeletal-disorder prevalence in a 545-person Thai office-worker study; neck, shoulders and back were the most common complaint areas.2
  • Frequent position change from sitting to standing or walking was associated with lower risk. That supports an active-break behavior—not claims that this clinic prevents or treats a disorder.2
  • Category familiarity: a Bangkok corporate provider currently lists Desk & Chair Yoga for 30 participants at ฿7,250.3
  • Venue adjacency: P23 presents itself around productivity, wellbeing and boutique wellness experiences. This indicates context, not purchase intent.4

Runner Reset 45

Leave able to perform one short routine at the right point around your run.

Product typeOne-off clinic
45 min total Max 8 runners ฿690 test price Thu 07:30 candidate

One date · one job

Edition AREADY

A dynamic 8–10 minute sequence for before a run. Avoid long passive holds when immediate performance is the goal.B

Edition BRESTORE

Controlled mobility and optional foam rolling for after a run or a non-running day. No recovery guarantee.B

Important: choose READY or RESTORE before sale. Trying to teach both in 45 minutes weakens the promised take-home result.

Why someone pays

Free running groups and online routines already exist. The product must add observation, correction and confident independent rehearsal.

Customer owns

One 8–10 minute routine card plus two personal cues they have already practiced.

It is for

Healthy recreational runners. Current pain, recent injury or post-surgery return-to-run is referred.

Reach first

Past run-event contacts with consent, current clients who run, then one partner club via one tracked link.

Portfolio comparison08

How to buy an answer cheaply

Test one product first.
Hold the offer still.

Economics before marketing Break-even seats = ceil((instructor + room/settlement + launch cost) ÷ (price − variable cost per person))

Paid launch gate = max(6, break-even seats + 2), with a hard capacity of 8. If the gate is above 8, the economics fail at this price.

1Choose by channel strength

Desk first only with committed P23/employer distribution. Runner first only with a usable warm list or partner.

2Sell one exact date

One promise, one slot, one price, full conditional payment, automatic refund if the gate is missed.

3Keep the variables stable

Do not change product, price and time together. A failed mixed test explains nothing.

4Score the whole visit

Paid seats, contribution, attendance, scope-fit inquiries, and whether participants can reproduce the routine.

Stop rule: after two comparable presale attempts below the gate, close the hypothesis. Do not rescue it with a last-minute discount or a recurring package.
