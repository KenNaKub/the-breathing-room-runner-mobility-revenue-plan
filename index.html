01 · Decision

Replace proven empty supply with one lower-friction course

Do not add a new 9:30 slot or ask one unproven cohort to attend twice weekly. Sell one four-week course into an existing empty Tuesday or Thursday class; add the second day only when it independently presells.

“Runners do not always need more stretching. They need the right mobility at the right time.”

One product, two routines

READY 10 is a short dynamic routine for before running. RESTORE 12 is controlled mobility, breathing and optional foam rolling for after a run or a non-running day. Both are modules inside Runner Mobility Lab—not separate public products.

Specialist adjustment: “RUN//RESET” was removed as the umbrella name because a Bangkok RE.SET community already combines women's running, stretching and mobility.20 Use the descriptive product name until a formal brand-name screen is completed.

03 · Market analysis

There is enough local evidence to justify a paid pilot, not enough to claim a large addressable market. The commercial wedge is people the studio or its partners can already reach, followed by nearby recreational runners with a specific race or routine need.

Priority 2 · Local

Priority 3 · Seasonal

Race-preparation runners

Major Bangkok race dates on 28–29 November 2026 and 10 January 2027 create legitimate campaign moments.89 Race entry does not prove willingness to attend weekday mornings.

Market thesis

Do not build for “all Bangkok runners.” Win a small, reachable wedge whose paid behavior can be measured.

The defensible white space

  • More specific than generic Pilates: runner-relevant ankles, calves, hips, trunk and single-leg control.
  • More valuable than a free warm-up: small-cohort feedback, progression and two repeatable routines.
  • Less clinical than physiotherapy: movement education for healthy recreational runners, with clear referral boundaries.
  • Lower friction than an eight-session commitment: one fixed session weekly for four weeks.
Market-size boundary: No reliable public source in this review quantifies the number of reachable runners who can attend these exact morning slots. Do not publish a fabricated TAM. Use consented contacts, deposits, conversion and renewal to measure the studio's serviceable market.

06 · Programming & claims

Dynamic before. Controlled reset after.

The product earns credibility by matching the method to the moment and refusing unsupported promises.

Take-home routine · approximately 10 minutes

READY 10

Dynamic movement and short isometric activation before running. Readiness without fatigue.

  1. Foot, ankle and calf movement.
  2. Controlled hip mobility.
  3. Glute, single-leg and trunk activation.
  4. Running-pattern rehearsal.
  5. No long passive holds immediately before running.
Take-home routine · approximately 12 minutes

RESTORE 12

Active and controlled mobility, breathing and optional foam rolling after or away from running.

  1. Foot, ankle and calf mobility.
  2. Hip rotation and extension.
  3. Thoracic rotation and breathing.
  4. Optional foam rolling.
  5. A short sequence that is easy to repeat independently.
Week 1 · Baseline

Non-clinical movement check-in; feet, ankles and calves.

Week 2 · Hips

Controlled rotation and extension; introduce RESTORE 12.

Week 3 · Control

Glute, trunk and single-leg control; introduce READY 10.

Week 4 · Integrate

Choose the right routine, repeat check-in and set continuation.

Evidence pointMarketing implication
Runner reviews report possible acute downsides from static stretching and support dynamic stretching when pre-run performance is the goal.1011Say: “Dynamic pre-run activation.” Avoid long passive holds in READY 10.
Foam rolling can improve acute range of motion and may support perceived recovery, but direct performance benefit is not established.1214Say: “Optional foam rolling for range and recovery perception.” Do not say: “Run faster.”
Routine static stretching has not been shown to reduce overall injury rates.15Do not say: “Prevents running injuries.”
Stretching and foam rolling were not superior to all other warm-up methods for acute range or stiffness in a 2024 review.16Sell the coaching, timing and repeatable routine—not a miracle technique.
Safety language: Runner Mobility Lab is fitness and movement education, not physiotherapy or medical treatment. Current pain, recent injury or surgery, neurological symptoms or medical restrictions should trigger referral to an appropriate healthcare professional.

Go / change / stop rules

  • Go at T−7: at least six fully paid and paid seats ≥ break-even + 2.
  • Scale: all eight places sold plus four deposit-ready prospects who cannot attend.
  • Renew: ≥75% attendance, ≥50% prepaid renewal and positive contribution per room hour.
  • Change or stop: two presale attempts fail to reach six fully paid runners.
  • Keep preview: ≥30% buy within seven days or the event is independently profitable.

These are proposed management rules, not industry benchmarks.

How Tuesday and Thursday scale
